Privacy Policy

We collect limited, anonymous data. Nothing is linked to you personally.

Effective date: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Water Eject ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by The App, Inc., handles information when you use the App. The App does not require an account, name, email, or any sign up. We collect a limited amount of anonymous data through third-party SDKs for product analytics, install attribution, and advertising measurement. None of this data is linked to your identity.

Information We Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, audio recordings, location, or any information you enter into the App. We collect the following limited, anonymous data:

  • Device & technical data — device model, operating system version, language, country/region, app version, time zone, coarse network type.
  • Usage data — app launches, basic interaction events (for example, "frequency played"), session duration, crash and performance diagnostics.
  • Install attribution data — anonymous identifiers used to measure which marketing campaign or referral brought you to the App.
  • Advertising identifiers — the Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you grant permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, no IDFA is collected.
  • IP address — collected briefly for fraud prevention and approximate region attribution, then discarded or truncated by our third-party providers per their policies.

This data is treated under Apple's "Data Not Linked to You" classification — it is not associated with your identity. We do not attempt to re-identify you from this data.

How the App Works

Water Eject is a sound frequency generator that plays tuned audio frequencies through your iPhone speaker. The frequency-playback feature works locally on your device and does not require an internet connection. An internet connection is used in the background to deliver anonymous attribution and analytics events to our service providers; this does not affect playback. We do not record audio and we do not use the microphone.

Permissions

Water Eject does not request access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal data on your device.

The first time you launch the App, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system will show a system prompt asking whether you allow tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can decline this prompt at any time, and you can change your choice later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, we do not collect your IDFA.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to collect the data described above. Each service receives only the data necessary for its purpose and is contractually required to handle it according to their own privacy policy:

  • AppsFlyer — mobile attribution and analytics. Used to measure marketing campaign performance, app installs, and aggregate usage events. Privacy policy: appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy.
  • Google Ads (Google LLC) — advertising measurement and conversion tracking for our marketing campaigns. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Apple — if you download the App from the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information about your download and use of the App. Apple's privacy practices are described at apple.com/legal/privacy.

We do not sell your data to data brokers. We do not share data with any third parties beyond the providers listed above.

Your Choices & Rights

You can control what is collected:

  • Decline tracking. Decline Apple's ATT prompt, or change your choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Limit ad tracking system-wide. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising lets you disable personalized ads at the system level.
  • Reset your advertising identifier. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Reset Advertising Identifier.
  • Delete the App. Removing the App from your device stops all future data collection.

If you are located in a region with applicable privacy law (such as the EU/EEA, UK, California, or Brazil), you may also have rights under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or LGPD — including the right to request access, deletion, or correction of any data we hold about you. Because our data is not linked to your identity, requests must include enough information for us or our providers to locate the relevant records. Contact support@theapp.biz to make a request.

Children's Privacy

Water Eject is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we do not collect names, emails, accounts, or any directly identifying information, the App contains no personally identifiable data tied to any user, including children. If you believe a child has provided information to us in any form, please contact support@theapp.biz and we will work with our providers to investigate and remove it.

Data Retention & Security

Anonymous analytics and attribution data is retained by our service providers (AppsFlyer, Google) for the periods described in their own privacy policies. We retain only aggregated reports derived from this data. Data in transit is protected using industry-standard TLS encryption.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be highlighted at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Water Eject, please contact us at support@theapp.biz.
