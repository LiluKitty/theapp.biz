Privacy Policy

Water Eject collects zero personal data. Period.

Effective date: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Water Eject ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by The App, Inc., handles information when you use the App. We have built Water Eject with privacy as a default — there is no account, no sign up, and no internet connection required.

Information We Collect

We collect zero personal data. Water Eject does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, audio recordings, contact information, device identifiers, location data, advertising identifiers, or analytics about you.

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any information to use any of its features.

How the App Works

Water Eject is a sound frequency generator that runs entirely on your device. It plays tuned audio frequencies through your iPhone speaker. The App does not record audio, does not use the microphone, and does not need an internet connection to function.

Permissions

Water Eject does not request access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal data on your device. The App only uses your device's standard audio output to play sound through the speaker, which does not require any special permission.

Third-Party Services

Water Eject does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-collection services. The App does not contain ads.

If you download the App through the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information about your download and use of the App. Apple's privacy practices are described at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Water Eject does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we do not collect personal data of any kind, the App is suitable for users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be highlighted at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Water Eject, please contact us at support@theapp.biz.
