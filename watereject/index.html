Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Water Eject

Is Water Eject free?
Yes, Water Eject is free to download and use, with no account or sign up. The core frequency-generator functionality is free. Certain advanced or specific features may be offered as an optional Premium in-app subscription, but all core features remain free. See our Terms of Use for subscription details.
How does Water Eject work?
Water Eject is a sound frequency generator. It plays tuned audio frequencies through your iPhone speaker. The vibration from these frequencies may help move trapped water or loose dust out of the speaker grill. Open the app, set your volume to maximum, point the speaker down, tap the button, and let the sound run.
Will Water Eject repair my water-damaged phone?
No. Water Eject does not claim to prevent water damage, it does not repair any kind of hardware damage, and it cannot undo harm that already happened to your device. If your phone has touched any liquid, please also follow Apple's official drying steps to take proper care of it.
Can Water Eject help with dust in the speaker?
The same kind of vibration that may move trapped water can also help loosen small dust and lint stuck in the speaker opening, which can sometimes affect how loud or clear your audio sounds.
Does Water Eject collect my data?
We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, audio, or location. We do collect a limited amount of anonymous data through AppsFlyer (install attribution and product analytics) and Google Ads (advertising measurement) — including device model, OS version, app events, and, only with your permission via Apple's ATT prompt, the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA). None of this is linked to your identity. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Does Water Eject need internet?
The core frequency-playback feature works offline — you can use it at the gym, by the pool, in the kitchen, or anywhere without signal. An internet connection is used in the background to deliver anonymous attribution and analytics events to our service providers, but this does not affect playback.
When should I use Water Eject?
Common moments include after rain, gym sweat, a splash in the kitchen, a wet pocket, or a quick fall into a sink or pool. It is also handy for testing your speakers or exploring how sound waves feel through your device.
What devices does Water Eject support?
Water Eject is built for iPhone.
