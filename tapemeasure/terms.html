What Tape Measure Is

Tape Measure is an augmented-reality (AR) measuring app for iPhone. The App uses your iPhone camera and Apple's ARKit framework to estimate distances, areas, heights, angles, and other dimensions in the real world. It includes an AR ruler, spirit level, area calculator, height measurement, 3D measurement, angle finder, TV sizer, and additional tools.

Important Disclaimer About Accuracy

Tape Measure provides approximate measurements only. The App uses computer vision and motion-tracking, which depend on lighting, surface texture, distance to the object, the quality of your device's camera and sensors, and how steady you hold the device. Results may vary between sessions, between devices, and even between two consecutive measurements of the same object.

Tape Measure is not a substitute for a physical measuring tool. Do not rely on the App for:

  • Construction, structural, engineering, or architectural work.
  • Legal, regulatory, real-estate, or contractual measurements.
  • Medical, fitness, or anatomical measurements.
  • Safety-critical decisions of any kind.
  • Cutting, drilling, welding, or any irreversible action where exact dimensions matter.

For any task where precision matters, please verify with a physical measuring tape, ruler, laser distance meter, or a licensed professional. You are responsible for verifying every measurement before acting on it.

Safe Use

Using the App involves pointing your iPhone camera at the real world and walking around. Please:

  • Be aware of your surroundings, do not measure while walking in traffic, on ladders, on stairs, near edges, or in any hazardous environment.
  • Hold your device securely with both hands when possible.
  • Do not use the App while driving or operating any vehicle or machinery.
  • Respect others' privacy, do not point the camera into private spaces or at people without consent.

You are responsible for using the App safely and in a way that does not harm yourself, others, or your device.
