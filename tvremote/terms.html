What TV Remote Is

TV Remote is a universal Smart TV remote for iPhone. The App connects to a compatible TV or streaming device on the same Wi-Fi network and lets you control volume, channels, power, and playback, navigate with a trackpad, type with your keyboard, and open the apps installed on your TV. It is designed to replace or supplement a physical remote control.

Compatibility & Requirements

TV Remote works with most popular Smart TVs and streaming devices, including Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku, Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV, TCL, Hisense, Philips, and Vizio. To work, your TV and your iPhone must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network, and your TV must support network control. Compatibility depends on your specific TV model, its firmware, and your network, and we cannot guarantee that every device or every feature will be supported.
