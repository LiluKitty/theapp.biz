Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TV Remote

Is TV Remote free?
TV Remote is free to download and try. It offers an optional Premium subscription that unlocks the full remote experience. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and can be managed or cancelled anytime in your Apple ID settings. See our Terms of Use for subscription details.
How do I connect my iPhone to my TV?
Connect your TV and your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi network, open TV Remote, select your TV from the list of detected devices, and pair. You can start controlling your TV in seconds.
Which TVs does TV Remote work with?
TV Remote works with most popular Smart TVs and streaming devices, including Samsung, LG, Sony, Roku, Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Vizio, and more. Compatibility depends on your specific TV model and network.
Can I open TV apps and type with a keyboard?
Yes. TV Remote gives you direct access to the apps installed on your TV, a trackpad for easy navigation, and a keyboard so you can search and type much faster than with the on-screen letters.
Does TV Remote need internet?
TV Remote controls your TV over your local Wi-Fi network, so your TV and iPhone need to be on the same network. Commands are sent directly to your TV on your local network.
Can I control more than one TV?
Yes. TV Remote supports multiple TVs, so you can pair and switch between the different TVs and streaming devices in your home.
Does TV Remote collect my data?
We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. TV Remote uses Local Network access only to find and control your TV, which stays on your device and local network. We collect a limited amount of anonymous data through AppsFlyer (attribution and analytics) and Google Ads (advertising measurement) that is not linked to your identity. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
What devices does TV Remote support?
TV Remote is built for iPhone.
