Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Solitaire

Is Solitaire free?
Yes, Solitaire is completely free to download and play, with no account or sign up and no subscription. The game is supported by ads, which lets us keep every feature free for everyone. See our Terms of Use for details.
How do I play Solitaire?
Solitaire uses the classic Klondike rules. Build the four foundations up from Ace to King by suit, and arrange cards in the tableau in descending order with alternating colors. Move cards, flip the deck, and clear the board to win. If you get stuck, tap for a hint or undo your last moves.
Does Solitaire have undo and hints?
Yes. You can undo as many moves as you like to experiment with different strategies, and you can tap for a smart hint whenever you are stuck and need to find your next move.
Does Solitaire have a subscription or in-app purchases?
No. Solitaire has no subscription. Every feature is free. The game is supported by standard in-app advertising through Google AdMob.
Does Solitaire collect my data?
We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. To show ads, the App uses Google AdMob, which may process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your permission via Apple's ATT prompt), IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Does Solitaire work offline?
Yes, you can play Solitaire offline. An internet connection is only used in the background to load ads and basic analytics; the game itself works without signal.
What devices does Solitaire support?
Solitaire is built for iPhone and iPad.
